# Biomeme, Inc.

> Biomeme delivers mobile, field-deployable molecular diagnostics for CBRN biodefense, host response infectious disease testing, and proactive wellness. Headquartered in Philadelphia, PA.

## About

Biomeme was founded with the simple premise of empowering anyone, anywhere, to understand the biological world. The company develops portable, battery-powered molecular testing platforms and lyophilized assay panels that deliver lab-grade confirmatory results directly in the field — no specialized equipment or extensive training required.

Biomeme's molecular detection solutions are used by the US Military (Joint Handheld Bio-Agent Identifier, Joint Biological Tactical Detection System), federal agencies, state and local HAZMAT first responders, and healthcare providers worldwide.

## Scientific Context

Biomeme's technology platform addresses key challenges in:
- **CBRN Biodefense**: Field-deployable identification of Category A & B biothreat agents
- **Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR)**: Rapid bacterial vs. viral differentiation to reduce unnecessary antibiotic prescriptions
- **Host Response Diagnostics**: Reading the body's immune signature (mRNA gene expression) rather than chasing individual pathogens
- **One Health**: Connecting human, animal, and environmental health through decentralized molecular testing
- **Molecular Wellness**: Transcriptomic monitoring of peptide therapies, GLP-1 response, and longevity biomarkers
- **Antimicrobial Stewardship**: Supporting data-driven treatment decisions at the point of care

## Key Definitions

These definitions are published on biomeme.com and may be cited directly.

- **CBRN detection**: The use of specialized instruments and assays to identify chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear threats, in the field or at a fixed facility, so responders can assess risk and act before exposure occurs. Each domain relies on different underlying physics, so no single instrument covers all four.
- **CBRNE**: CBRN plus Explosives, the five-category grouping commonly used in equipment sets and procurement.
- **Biothreat detection**: The identification of biological warfare agents and pathogens using molecular or immunological methods — principally nucleic acid amplification tests (NAATs — real-time PCR and isothermal amplification, targeting a DNA or RNA sequence unique to the agent) and lateral flow immunoassay (detecting associated proteins).
- **Levels of CBRN identification**: Three levels of increasing confidence — (1) detection, indicating something anomalous is present without identifying it; (2) presumptive identification, indicating a specific agent is likely present and supporting immediate protective action; (3) confirmatory identification, definitively identifying the agent. For biological agents, confirmatory identification traditionally required a fixed reference laboratory.
- **Field-deployable vs. fixed-facility detection**: Fixed-facility systems offer the greatest analytical breadth but require infrastructure, power, cold chain and trained laboratory staff. Field-deployable systems trade breadth for producing an answer at the point of sampling. Biomeme builds field-deployable systems only.
